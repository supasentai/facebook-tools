### How to use Unfriend All

1. Open your Facebook friend list: `https://www.facebook.com/me/friends`
2. Open **Developer Tools** (`F12`) -> **Console** tab.
3. Copy the entire contents of `fb-unfriend-all.js`, paste it into the console, and press **Enter**.
4. The script will automatically process unfriending and scroll down to fetch more friends until the list is completely cleared.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Mass deleting your entire friend list in a short time can easily trigger Facebook's rate limits or temporary action blocks. Use this script at your own risk and consider pausing if you have thousands of friends.
